ABOUT CHUTTI CINEMA

Education Beyond Textbooks

Cinema is more than entertainment. It can be a powerful medium for education, self-discovery and understanding society.

Chutti Cinema brings cinema into the educational environment through structured screening sessions, interactive discussions and student-led creative projects. Our approach connects visual storytelling with real-life experiences, helping students develop curiosity, critical thinking, empathy and an understanding of diverse cultures and communities.

01

Think Critically

Encourage questions, curiosity and independent thinking.

02

Understand Others

Explore diverse perspectives, cultures and communities.

03

Express Creatively

Discover the confidence to share ideas through storytelling.

OUR VISION

Inspire Curious Minds.

OUR MISSION

Make Every Story Matter.

To make cinema a meaningful educational resource by encouraging students to think critically, understand life from different perspectives and develop the confidence to tell their own stories.

THE CHUTTI CINEMA JOURNEY

Our Programme

A Six-Session Cinema-Based Learning Journey

A structured learning experience across the academic year, combining film appreciation, guided discussions, documentary learning and hands-on filmmaking.

01 FILM APPRECIATION

Indian Cinema Screening & Discussion

Explore selected Indian films that introduce meaningful themes from everyday life, society, culture and human relationships.

✦ Reflect on characters, choices and values.
02 GLOBAL PERSPECTIVES

International Cinema Screening & Discussion

Discover films from around the world that introduce different cultures, lifestyles, social experiences and perspectives.

✦ Build cultural understanding and a broader worldview.
03 DISCOVERY & RESEARCH

Documentary Screening & Expert Discussion

Watch documentaries on science, nature, history, the environment and society, followed by expert-led discussions.

✦ Ask questions, examine evidence and connect ideas.
04 CREATIVE SKILLS

Documentary Making Class

Learn how to choose a subject, research a topic, develop a story, prepare interview questions and plan shots. Discover basic camera, sound and editing techniques.

✦ Learn the fundamentals of visual storytelling.
05 HANDS-ON PROJECT

Student Documentary Project

Students create their own short documentaries through research, information gathering, suitable interviews and visual storytelling, with appropriate guidance.

✦ Turn ideas and research into original stories.
Local History Culture Environment Science
06 FINAL SHOWCASE

Student Documentary Competition & Showcase

Students present their completed documentary projects at a school-level showcase or competition, celebrating creativity, teamwork, research and storytelling.

✦ Share stories, celebrate ideas and learn from peers.
◷

Learning at the Right Pace

The programme is designed to run across the academic year. Adequate time is provided between the filmmaking class and the final showcase for students to research, film and complete their documentary projects.

LEARNING BEYOND THE SCREEN

Cinema & Life Skills

Learning Values Through Stories

Stories help students explore situations, understand people and think about the choices they make. Through age-appropriate films and guided discussions, Chutti Cinema encourages students to reflect on important life skills and social themes.

01

Empathy & Understanding

Appreciate the feelings and experiences of others and learn to see situations from different perspectives.

02

Courage & Resilience

Understand how people face challenges, overcome setbacks and find the strength to keep moving forward.

03

Ethics & Responsibility

Reflect on personal choices, values, responsibilities and the consequences of our actions.

04

Equality & Inclusion

Recognise the dignity of every person and value diversity, fairness and respect for others.

05

Environmental Awareness

Explore the connection between people and nature and reflect on our responsibility towards the planet.

06

Teamwork & Collaboration

Discover how cooperation, listening and shared responsibility help people achieve common goals.

07

Critical Thinking

Ask meaningful questions, examine ideas and develop independent opinions through guided reflection.

08

Cultural Understanding

Discover different communities, traditions and ways of life through stories from around the world.

STUDENT DOCUMENTARY INITIATIVE

From Watching Stories to Telling Stories

Chutti Cinema encourages students to move beyond watching films and become creators of their own stories.

Through guided documentary projects, students discover meaningful stories in their schools, neighbourhoods and communities. They learn to research, ask questions, interview people responsibly and communicate information through visual storytelling.
